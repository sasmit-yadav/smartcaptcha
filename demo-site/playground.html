🌍 Eco Tip of the Day

Did you know? Switching to LED bulbs can reduce your home lighting energy by up to 75%. A single LED bulb lasts 25x longer than incandescent and saves about $80 in electricity costs over its lifetime. Small changes add up — if every household replaced just 5 bulbs, it would prevent 90 billion pounds of greenhouse gas emissions annually.
